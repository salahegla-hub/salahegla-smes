> **Note.** This is a **methodological framework** illustrating how the analysis and its outputs work. It is not a report on a specific client engagement. No client names or actual project figures are presented here.

Tourism projects are among the hardest to appraise, because their revenue rests on two highly volatile variables: **occupancy rate** and **average spend per visitor**. An error of two percentage points in occupancy can turn a viable project into a loss-making one.

The framework therefore rests on a single principle: **no figure without its basis, and no result without a sensitivity test**. Demand estimates are built bottom-up, the result is tested across three scenarios, and the variables that actually decide the outcome are identified explicitly — so verification effort concentrates on them before capital is committed.
