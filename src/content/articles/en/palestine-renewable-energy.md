## Abstract

High population growth, rising living standards and rapid industrial growth have produced **enormous demand for energy** in the Palestinian territories. Against the problem of energy security, attention has increasingly turned to greater reliance on renewable sources.

This article sets out to examine **how renewable energy use has developed in Palestine and the challenges facing it**.

## The energy picture

- Under the **Oslo Accords**, any development in electricity or energy must proceed through cooperation between Palestine and Israel — a structural constraint on any independent development path.
- The electricity sector depends on Israel for **86%** of its electrical supply, alongside further challenges in infrastructure and distribution.
