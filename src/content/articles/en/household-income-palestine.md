> **Unpublished working paper**, prepared during postgraduate study at the Doha Institute for Graduate Studies. The full text is in Arabic and available as a PDF from the top of this page.

## Research question

Economic, political and security conditions are among the most significant factors shaping the lives of Palestinian individuals and households. Against a background of sustained economic blockade, political conflict and continuous security threats, the study addresses a specific question:

> **How far do economic, political and security conditions affect the monthly income of Palestinian households?**

## Data and method

The study uses data from the **2022 Arab Index**, applying econometric models with **controls for demographic and geographic factors** to analyse how these conditions affect household income across different areas of Palestine.

## Findings

- A household's **economic situation has a positive effect on monthly income**: respondents who described their household's economic position as good or very good report higher monthly incomes.
- **Average monthly expenditure is the only variable with an effective influence on income**, owing to the very close correspondence between expenditure and income — around **78%** — which in practice means households spend almost everything they earn, with negligible margin for saving.

## Framing

The paper draws on prior literature including UNDP reporting (2020), which found that economic conditions in Palestine contribute to widening disparity between households, deepening poverty and narrowing economic opportunity.
